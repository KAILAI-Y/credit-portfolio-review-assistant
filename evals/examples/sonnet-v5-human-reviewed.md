## Executive Summary

This review examines a historical sample of Taiwanese credit card clients drawn from the April–September 2005 repayment history, with a next-month default-payment outcome label (source: dataset_context). Across the full portfolio of 30,000 clients, 6,636 carried a positive default label, for an overall label rate of 22.12% (source: portfolio_overview). As the dataset context notes, this is a historical 2005 sample and should not be read as an estimate of current credit risk (source: dataset_context). The sections below summarize label-rate patterns across repayment-status groups and credit-limit bands, compare two candidate review scopes, and flag key data-quality and interpretive limitations.

## Key Findings

Segmenting by repayment-status group, "PAY_0 <= 0" comprised 23,182 clients (77.27% portfolio share) with a label rate of 13.83% and covered 48.33% of all positive labels (source: repayment_status_summary). "PAY_0 = 1" comprised 3,688 clients (12.29% portfolio share) with a label rate of 33.95% and 18.87% positive-label coverage (source: repayment_status_summary). "PAY_0 >= 2" comprised 3,130 clients (10.43% portfolio share) with a label rate of 69.55% and 32.81% positive-label coverage (source: repayment_status_summary).

By credit-limit band (fixed exploratory bands based on the original sample's quartiles, not bank policy thresholds) (source: credit_limit_band_definitions), label rates ranged from 31.79% in the "<= 50,000" band down to 13.98% in the "> 240,000" band, with portfolio shares roughly even across bands (25.59%, 25.38%, 25.48%, 23.56% respectively) (source: credit_limit_summary).

Within the stratified breakdown by repayment-status group and credit-limit band, the highest label rate observed was 70.74% for the "PAY_0 >= 2" group in the "50,000-140,000" band, and the lowest was 9.89% for the "PAY_0 <= 0" group in the "> 240,000" band (source: computed_facts).

The PAY_0 column also contains undocumented codes retained without an assigned interpretation: code -2 with 2,759 clients, and code 0 with 14,737 clients (source: computed_facts).

## Review-Scope Trade-offs

Two candidate review scopes were compared. Scope A ("PAY_0 >= 2") covers 3,130 clients, a 10.43% workload share, a label rate of 69.55%, and 32.81% positive-label coverage (source: review_scope_comparison). Scope B ("PAY_0 > 0"), which contains Scope A as a subset (source: review_scope_comparison), covers 6,818 clients, a 22.73% workload share, a label rate of 50.29%, and 51.67% positive-label coverage (source: review_scope_comparison).

Expanding from Scope A to Scope B adds 3,688 incremental clients and 1,252 incremental positive labels, with an incremental-population label rate of 33.95%, and produces a coverage gain of 18.87 percentage points (source: review_scope_increment; source: computed_facts).

These review scopes were assessed only on the same historical sample used for this exploration; their performance on new clients or later periods is unknown (source: limitations). They are exploratory rules and should not be described as approved bank policy (source: limitations).

## Limitations

Scope A and Scope B are exploratory groupings, not approved bank policies (source: limitations). PAY_0 codes 0 and -2 are undocumented in the reviewed source description and are retained without an invented interpretation (source: limitations; source: data_quality). All segment and scope comparisons in this report describe historical statistical associations only, not causal effects, and do not establish that reviewing a client or changing a credit limit changes default risk (source: limitations). A rate or coverage value is undefined only when its own denominator is zero - a segment with zero clients has an undefined label rate, and a portfolio with zero positive labels overall has undefined positive-label coverage - and is reported as null rather than as zero; a segment with a nonzero client count but zero positive labels instead has a real, defined label rate of 0% (source: limitations). Finally, this dataset reflects a 2005 historical period and is not a current risk estimate (source: dataset_context).
